## Core Concepts

### Robotics

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Forward kinematics | Compute end-effector pose from joint angles |
| Inverse kinematics | Compute joint angles from end-effector pose |
| Dynamics | Relationship between forces/torques and motion |

### State Estimation

- **Kalman filter**: optimal estimation for linear Gaussian systems
- **Particle filter**: approximate estimation for nonlinear, non-Gaussian systems
- **Sensor fusion**: combining multiple sources to improve robustness
